# Legal officer

**You do:** disputes, liens, court orders, and checking transfers.

1. From a parcel's **Legal** card: record a dispute, register a lien or mortgage, or record a court order.
2. An open dispute, a freeze order or an active lien blocks transfers and subdivision; the card shows why.
3. `/disputes` lists open disputes, active liens, court orders and parcel changes waiting for a decision.
4. Closing a dispute, releasing a lien or lifting an order needs a second officer and a written reason.

**Exercises:** register a lien, try a transfer (blocked), have a colleague release it, then transfer.
