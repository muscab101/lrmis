# Surveyor

**You do:** walk or draw the boundary, record buildings and neighbours.

1. Your work is in `/surveys` (assigned to you) and on the dashboard ("Surveys assigned to you").
2. Open a survey → **Draw**, or walk the corners with GPS (accuracy is recorded), or import a file. Corners snap to existing parcels so edges line up.
3. Fill in neighbours (north/east/south/west), the building and photos. **Save** often; you can finish later.
4. **Submit.** If the shape overlaps another parcel, it goes to GIS review; the overlap is shown in red.
5. **Subdivisions** start from a parcel's Legal card → Subdivide: draw each new plot inside the outline. They must not overlap and must cover the parent within 1 %.

**Exercises:** survey a plot by drawing, fix an overlap that comes back from review, draw a two-way subdivision.
