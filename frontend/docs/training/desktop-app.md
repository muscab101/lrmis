# LRMIS app for your computer

For everyone who views or prints certificates and ID cards.

LRMIS works in Chrome and Firefox as before. The **LRMIS app** is the same LRMIS in its own window, and that window is
protected: screenshots, screen recording and screen sharing (Zoom, Meet, Teams, AnyDesk) of it come out **black**.
Open certificates in the app whenever you can.

## Install it (once)

### Mac

1. Open the installer you were given: `LRMIS-1.0.0-arm64.dmg` (newer Macs with an Apple chip: M1, M2, M3, M4) or
   `LRMIS-1.0.0.dmg` (older Intel Macs). Not sure? Apple menu → About This Mac → "Chip" or "Processor".
2. Drag **LRMIS** into the **Applications** folder.
3. Open Applications and **right-click LRMIS → Open**, then **Open** again. If the Mac still says it can't be opened:
   System Settings → Privacy & Security → scroll down → **Open Anyway**. This is needed only the first time.

### Windows

1. Open the installer you were given: `LRMIS Setup 1.0.0.exe`.
2. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC": click **More info → Run anyway**.
3. Follow the steps. LRMIS appears on the desktop and in the Start menu.

## Use it

1. Open **LRMIS** from Applications (Mac) or the desktop / Start menu (Windows).
2. **Sign in** with your usual LRMIS email, password and 2FA code. The app keeps its own sign-in: signing in to Chrome
   does not sign you in here.
3. Work as usual. Certificates, ID cards and printing are under **Documents**.
4. Links to other websites open in your normal browser.

Good to know:

- A screenshot or screen share of the app is black. That is on purpose: don't try to send certificates as pictures.
  The owner's proof is the printed certificate, and anyone can check it with its QR code.
- In Chrome or Firefox the certificate turns black when the screenshot keys are pressed, but other screenshot tools can
  still copy it there. Use the app for certificates.
- Zoom in or out: View menu (or Cmd/Ctrl and + / −). Full screen: View → Toggle Full Screen.

## Problems

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The app opens a blank or error page | Check the internet, then View → Reload. |
| "Session expired" | Sign in again. |
| Asked to sign in every day | Normal: sessions end after a time without use. |
| Mac says the app is damaged or can't be opened | Use right-click → Open, or Privacy & Security → Open Anyway (see above). |

# Barnaamijka LRMIS ee kombiyuutarka

Qof kasta oo arka ama daabaca shahaadooyinka iyo kaararka aqoonsiga.

LRMIS wuxuu ku shaqeeyaa Chrome iyo Firefox sidii hore. **Barnaamijka LRMIS** waa isla LRMIS oo daaqad u gaar ah ku
jira, daaqaddaasna waa la ilaaliyaa: sawir-qaadista shaashadda, duubista iyo wadaagista shaashadda (Zoom, Meet, Teams,
AnyDesk) waxay ku soo baxaan **madow**. Shahaadooyinka ku fur barnaamijka mar kasta oo aad awoodo.

## Ku rakib (hal mar)

**Mac:** fur faylka la siiyay (`LRMIS-1.0.0-arm64.dmg` Mac-yada cusub ee Apple chip, ama `LRMIS-1.0.0.dmg` Mac-yada
Intel), u jiid **LRMIS** galka **Applications**, kadib **midig-guji LRMIS → Open → Open**. Haddii Mac-gu diido:
System Settings → Privacy & Security → **Open Anyway**.

**Windows:** fur `LRMIS Setup 1.0.0.exe`. Haddii uu soo baxo "Windows protected your PC": **More info → Run anyway**,
kadib raac tallaabooyinka.

## Isticmaal

1. Fur **LRMIS** (Applications ama desktop-ka / Start).
2. **Gal** adigoo isticmaalaya email-kaaga, furahaaga iyo koodhka 2FA. Barnaamijku wuxuu leeyahay gelitaan u gaar ah.
3. U shaqee sidii caadiga ahayd. Shahaadooyinka iyo daabacaadda waxay ku jiraan **Documents**.

Sawirka shaashadda ee barnaamijka waa madow: taasi waa ula kac. Caddaynta milkiilaha waa shahaadada la daabacay, qof
kastaana wuu ku hubin karaa koodhkeeda QR.
