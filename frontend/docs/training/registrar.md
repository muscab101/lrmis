# Land officer / registrar

**You do:** verify, publish notices, register, issue certificates, approve changes.

1. **Verify** an application with the checklist. You can't verify a survey you made.
2. **Public notice** runs 14 days and closes automatically. Objections block registration until resolved.
3. **Register:** the parcel gets its PUN (`BN-<district>-<neighbourhood>-<number>`) and owners.
4. **Certificates** (`/documents/print`): only when nothing is owed and there is no dispute. Printing is logged once; a reprint needs a reason, a fee and a second person.
5. **Transfers, subdivisions, merges and change of use** are approved by a second officer. Check the stamp duty is paid before approving a transfer.

**Exercises:** verify and register a test application; issue and print a certificate; approve a colleague's transfer; reject one with a reason.
