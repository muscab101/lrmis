# Cashier

**You do:** take payments and close your shift.

1. **Open your shift** at `/cashier` with the opening float.
2. Find the bill by invoice number or PUN. Take cash or mobile money and print the receipt. The payer gets an SMS receipt.
3. Money without a bill (overpayment) stays as credit on the parcel; it is applied to the next bill.
4. **Close your shift:** count the cash and enter it. Any difference is recorded and reviewed by finance. Never cover a difference from your own pocket.
5. **Mistakes:** you can't delete a payment. Ask finance for a reversal with the reason (Payments → Reversals).

**Exercises:** open a shift, take three payments (one partial, one overpaid), close with a 1 USD difference.
